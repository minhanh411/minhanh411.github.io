# Welcome to My Site

## About Me

I come from a business and operations background — not technical by training, but genuinely curious about how data, machine learning, and technology actually work under the hood.

This site is where I think out loud. When I learn something new, I try to write it down in plain language — the way I wish someone had explained it to me. No jargon for the sake of it, no pretending I know more than I do.

Beyond the technical side, I learn writting — essays, short reflections, whatever's on my mind. And I have projects, some technical, some not and whatever rabbit hole I've fallen into lately.

If you're also somewhere in the middle — not quite technical, not quite not — you might feel at home here.
